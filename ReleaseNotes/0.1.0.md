The first release of Tinycap: a small menu-bar recorder for short screen clips.

- Record an area, a window or a whole screen from the menu bar or with keyboard shortcuts.
- Preview and trim the clip before saving.
- Export as MP4 (H.264 or HEVC), GIF (sized to fit a limit you pick) or WebM (VP9).
- Recordings are saved to ~/Movies/Tinycap, or a folder you choose.
