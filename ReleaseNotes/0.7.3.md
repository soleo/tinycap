A fix for recordings lost when quitting.

- **Quitting while recording keeps the recording:** if you quit Tinycap without stopping a recording, it now finishes saving first and offers the recording the next time you open Tinycap. Before, that recording could not be opened.
- **Clearer prompt for recordings that can't be opened:** a recording cut off by a crash or a force quit is now offered for deletion, or shown in Finder, instead of opening an empty preview.
