Recordings can now have sound.

- **System audio:** record everything your Mac plays, in window, area and screen recordings. Tinycap's own sounds are left out.
- **Microphone:** talk over your recording. Pick any input (built-in, USB, an audio interface or Bluetooth) from the Microphone menu, the bar under an area selection, or Settings → Recording. macOS asks for Microphone access the first time you turn it on.
- **No speaker echo:** the microphone goes through the same echo cancellation as FaceTime, so sound from your speakers doesn't play twice. Turn off "Reduce echo from speakers" in Settings for a studio mic or headphones.
- **Preview with sound:** play the recording with sound, and untick System audio or Microphone to leave either out.
- **MP4 keeps the sound,** mixed into one track so it plays everywhere, including Chrome, Slack and GitHub. Tick "Separate tracks for editing" to keep system audio and the microphone apart for a video editor. WebM and GIF stay silent.
- Sound is off until you turn it on, so recordings and file sizes don't change otherwise.
